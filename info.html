Food Fight - Information

Welcome to Food Fight!

Food Fight is a fun game where you, the user, can fight it out for your favorite foods!

The score underneath each food represents the popularity of that food among players.

Clicking on a food will increase its score and bring it nearer to the podium!

On the podium, you can see the top 3 most popular foods based on the scores.

Have fun playing and discovering new favorite foods!
